about the
brand.

sugar bloom is a passion project built around the idea that ice cream should be just as fun to look at as it is to eat. blending nostalgic charm with a playful, modern aesthetic, the brand features bold typography, a refreshing color palette, signature stripes, and whimsical graphic elements. the identity extends across packaging, print, signage, and digital experiences, creating a cohesive visual world that's sweet, inviting, and full of personality.
