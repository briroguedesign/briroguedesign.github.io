hi, i’m bri.

I’m a graphic designer with a BFA from Michigan State University, a love for clean visuals, and a slight obsession with cars, branding, and making things look cooler than they probably need to be.

Design has been part of my life for as long as I can remember. What started as random posters and character art turned into full-on brand systems, packaging, signage, digital campaigns, and everything in between. Whether it’s a startup looking for a full identity or a one-off passion project, I love turning ideas into visuals that actually feel like something.
