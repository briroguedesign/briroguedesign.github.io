the michigan state university racing club brings together a community of students united by their passion for cars, motorsports, and automotive culture. the rebrand was designed to capture the energy and individuality of that community while staying connected to its spartan roots. combining bold typography, motorsport-inspired graphic elements, and a cohesive visual identity, the project creates a recognizable brand that translates across digital media, merchandise, and promotional materials.
