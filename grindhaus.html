grindhaus is a passion project designed to break away from the predictable aesthetics of the automotive detailing industry. inspired by car culture and contemporary graphic design, the identity combines bold typography, striking contrast, and an unapologetic visual attitude. the goal was to create something that goes beyond a detailing service — a distinctive brand that celebrates the passion, precision, and personality behind automotive ownership.
